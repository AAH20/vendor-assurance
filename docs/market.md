# Market hypothesis and buyer validation

Initial problem: before a critical cloud, SaaS or AI supplier renews, reconcile conflicting claims, stale evidence and unresolved commitments into a reviewable decision. Target a named third-party-risk or operational-risk owner, with procurement and security participating. Market category evidence is not proof of willingness to buy this implementation.

Sources reviewed September 2026:

- [Verizon 2026 DBIR](https://www.verizon.com/about/news/breach-industry-wide-dbir-finds): third-party involvement is material; breach prevalence is not proof this product prevents breaches.
- [EBA register-of-information Q&A](https://www.eba.europa.eu/single-rule-book-qa/qna/view/publicId/2024_7098): illustrates ongoing ICT contractual information obligations for relevant financial entities. This project does not determine applicability or certify DORA compliance.
- [ServiceNow TPRM](https://www.servicenow.com/products/third-party-risk-management.html): established lifecycle, monitoring and remediation alternative. Compete only where a customer measures a benefit beyond configuration of existing tools.
- [Ironclad product descriptions](https://legal.ironcladapp.com/fy2026-product-descriptions): obligation and renewal capabilities already exist; generic summaries are not differentiation.
- [Vanta questionnaire automation](https://www.vanta.com/products/questionnaire-automation): established answer-generation alternative. Our initial hypothesis is buyer-side evidence reconciliation.
- [CISO Assistant Community](https://github.com/intuitem/ciso-assistant-community): broad OSS GRC/TPRM alternative. Integrate rather than duplicate its generic register; examine AGPL obligations before code reuse.

## First commercial experiment

Recruit three separately consenting design partners. Establish actual monthly review volume, active minutes per review, correction rates, backlog, tools already paid for and named budget owner. Obtain permission for a representative limited evidence set. Run a fixed-scope paid evaluation against their existing process. Do not assume large regulated enterprises are accessible merely because their budgets are large.

Stop or revise if the customer can meet the need by configuring an incumbent, will not authorize representative evidence, has infrequent reviews, or cannot show a budgeted owner. Product differentiation must be measured as trustworthy evidence handling and lower reviewer effort across existing systems.

OSS self-operation, assisted deployment, managed deployment and managed support are possible packaging tiers. No market-average price or accepted price is claimed. Quote after measuring vendor portfolio, ingestion/integration effort, isolation requirements and support coverage. Do not promise unlimited review or intolerable-error guarantees.

Search-intent phrases: vendor risk assessment, third-party risk management, vendor due diligence, contract obligation tracking, supplier renewal review, compliance evidence management. Search volume has not been measured; these are descriptive keyword hypotheses, not verified high-volume rankings.

The future marketplace may route an approved remediation request to a specialist, then verify agreed deliverables. Commercial placement must not influence assurance findings; disclose supplier relationships. Community pilot participation does not authorize commercial profiling or outreach.
