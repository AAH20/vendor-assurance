# Publication boundary

Public: source, schemas expressed by sample bundles, synthetic examples, tests, limitations, general architecture, evaluation definitions and A2Z SOC attribution.

Private: customer contracts, reports, identities, grants, supplier disputes, customer-specific weaknesses, negotiated prices, incident details, reviewer notes, real evaluation bundles and commercial meetings. Generated evidence exports contain original documents: do not publish them unless every included source is authorized for publication.

The synthetic example and decision are fictitious. No CNCF endorsement, administrator authorization or installation is claimed. No DMs/private-channel ingestion, automated outreach, sanctions, payments or contract execution.
